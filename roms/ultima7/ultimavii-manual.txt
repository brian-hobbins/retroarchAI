Getting Started

Insert the Game Pak into the Super Nintendo Entertainment System and power on. After the introduction sequence, the title screen appears. If a save file exists, options to Resume Saved Game or Begin New Game will be available. Use the directional keypad to highlight an option and press the B Button to select.
Game Controls
Controller Mapping

    Control Pad: Moves the Avatar.
    A Button: Uses the item in the A-hand.
    B Button: Uses the item in the B-hand.
    Y Button: Opens doors, inspects containers, retrieves items, and initiates conversation.
    START: Opens the Inventory Screen.
    SELECT: Opens the Game Control Screen.
    L/R Buttons: Casts the assigned Fast Magic spell.

Inventory & Gameplay

Press START to view the inventory. The screen displays the character portrait, equipped items, and backpack contents. Use the cursor to select items and press A or B to equip them to the respective hand. Press Y to use an item.

Conversation Mode: Press Y to initiate dialogue. Use the Control Pad to highlight keywords and press B to speak them. Look for a downward-pointing arrow to see more text.
Spell Casting

After obtaining the Spellbook from Lord British, access it via the inventory screen. Use the Y Button to open the book and the Control Pad to select a spell. Press Y to cast.

Fast Magic: Within the Spellbook, select a spell and press the L or R button to assign it to that button for quick casting during gameplay.
Spell List

    Explosion: Creates a blast in front of the caster.
    Healing/Great Heal: Restores health.
    Illumination: Creates a light source.
    Invincibility/Invisibility: Provides protection or stealth.
    Kal Lor (Help): Teleports the caster to Lord British for healing.
    Levitation: Allows movement over impassable terrain.
    Lifting: Moves large objects.
    Locate: Provides an aerial view of Britannia.
    Ring of Fire: Damages enemies in a circle.
    Teleportation: Creates a return token.
    Tremors: Causes earthquakes to damage enemies.
    Unlock Door/Magic: Opens locked doors.
    Water Walk: Allows walking on water surfaces.

Guidance from Batlin

(Summary) The manual includes a narrative section titled "Guidance from Batlin," which provides lore regarding the history of Britannia, the formation of "The Fellowship," and the philosophical "Triad of Inner Strength" (Strive for Unity, Trust Thy Brother, Worthiness Precedes Reward). It serves as background context for the game's story.
