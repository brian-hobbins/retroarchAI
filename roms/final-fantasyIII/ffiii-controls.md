# Final Fantasy III (SNES, USA) — Player Controls

This game has two modes of play: **traveling** (walking the field/towns/dungeons,
menus) and **fighting** (turn-based battles). The same buttons do different things
in each mode. Press only the buttons listed here; do not guess a control.

## Button Map — Traveling (field, towns, menus)

- **Control Pad**: Move your party across the field / dungeons / towns. Move the
  cursor on menu and subscreen screens. Also steers Chocobos and Airships.
- **A Button**: MAIN ACTION / CONFIRM. Talk to villagers, open treasure chests,
  push switches/examine things, and confirm the selected choice in any menu.
  (Move the cursor to the option with the Control Pad, then press A.)
  **A also advances a dialogue/message box** that is waiting for you — press A to
  step through a conversation one box at a time. (A cutscene that plays on its
  own needs no input; only press A when a box is sitting still waiting.)
- **B Button**: CANCEL. Cancels the last choice / backs out of a menu. Also used
  to climb off a Chocobo or the Airship.
- **X Button**: Open the **subscreen** (the main menu) while in the field or a town.
- **Y Button**: In multi-party sections, switch from one party to the next. In a
  spell list, shows the Magic Points (MP) needed for each spell.
- **L / R Buttons**: In menu screens with long lists, scroll through the list.
- **START**: Turn the field map on/off while traveling. Also completes name entry.
- **SELECT**: In menu screens with long lists, jump-scroll a whole screen at a time.

## Button Map — Fighting (battle)

When a fighter's ATB gauge fills, their command list appears (Fight / Skill /
Magic / Item). Choose with the Control Pad + A.

- **Control Pad**: Move the cursor to pick a command, a spell/item, or a target.
  - **Left** on the Control Pad = send that fighter to the **back row** (Row).
  - **Right** on the Control Pad = **Defend** (defensive stance this turn).
  - **Up** in the Magic menu = access equipped **Espers** (Esper Magic).
  - **Up** in the Item list = manage/equip Weapons from the list.
- **A Button**: Confirm the selected command / spell / item / target.
- **B Button**: Cancel the last choice (step back a menu level).
- **X Button**: Skip a party member (pass their current turn).
- **Y Button**: In multi-party battles, switch control to the next party. Also
  shows MP cost in the spell list.
- **L or R Button**: Make the chosen spell affect **all** enemies.
- **L + R together (hold both)**: Attempt to **run** from the battle.
- **START**: Pause the battle ("breather").

### Battle commands (selected with A)
- **Fight**: Attack the targeted enemy with the equipped Weapon.
- **Special Skill** (varies by character): e.g. SwdTech (Cyan), Blitz (Sabin —
  press a series of buttons), Lore (Strago), Rage (Gau), Dance (Mog), Steal/Capture
  (Locke), Tools (Edgar), etc.
- **Magic**: Choose a learned spell from the list, then pick a target.
- **Item**: Use an item on a chosen party member or enemy.

## Name Input (when a character is introduced)
- **Control Pad**: Move the cursor over the alphabet grid.
- **A Button**: Confirm the highlighted letter (adds it to the name).
- **B Button**: Erase a letter / move back one space.
- **START**: Finish — keep the name when you are happy with it. (Names are 6
  letters or fewer. You may keep the preset name by pressing START immediately.)

## Vehicles

### Chocobo (ground mount; no random encounters while riding)
- **Up**: Move forward. **Left / Right**: Turn.
- **A Button**: Move forward. **B Button**: Dismount (it runs back to its stable).

### Airship (world travel; no encounters while riding)
- **Up / Down**: Adjust altitude. **Left / Right**: Turn.
- **A Button**: Move forward. **B Button**: Leave the Airship (land/disembark).
- **X Button**: Switch view to the inside (go to the deck/interior action).
- **Y Button**: Turn the Airship while it is not moving.
- **L / R Buttons**: Make high-speed turns.

## Subscreen & Saving
- Press **X** to open the subscreen. Options: Item, Skills, Equip, Relic, Status,
  Config, Save (move cursor with Control Pad, confirm with A).
- **Save**: Choose "Save" on the subscreen — available any time in the field, and
  at Save Points (glowing tiles). Highlighted = saving is allowed here.
- **Order the party**: In the subscreen party list, press **Left** to raise the
  cursor, press A on one fighter then A on another to swap their positions.
- **Front / Back row**: Point the cursor at a fighter and press **A twice** to
  toggle them between the front row (full damage dealt/taken) and back row (about
  half damage dealt/taken).

## Config options worth knowing (Config on the subscreen)
- **Bat. Mode**: Active vs **Wait**. On "Wait", enemies do NOT act while you are in
  the Item/Magic menus — safer for careful, deliberate play.
- **Bat. Speed / Msg. Speed**: Battle pace and text-print speed (1–6).
- **Cmd. Set**: Window (default) or Short command layout.
- **Gauge**: On = show ATB power bars; Off = show current HP instead.
- **Cursor**: Reset vs Memory (Memory makes the cursor remember its last position,
  so repeated A confirms the same action).

## Quick reference (most-used)
- Talk / open / confirm = **A**. Cancel / back = **B**. Main menu = **X**.
- Move = **Control Pad**. Save = **X → Save**.
- In battle: pick command/target with Control Pad + **A**; run = hold **L + R**;
  hit all enemies with a spell = press **L or R**.
